Privacy Policy — Pippet

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Thank you for playing Pippet. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: Pippet does not collect, store, or share any personal data, and nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

  • None. Pippet does not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or any other personal details to play.

Health & activity data

With your permission, Pippet reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit), entirely on your device:

  • Step count — turned into food and energy for your virtual pet, and into little treasures it "finds" on your walks.
  • Workouts (start and end times only) — so your pet can cheer and "train" with you.
  • Activity rings (progress toward your Move, Exercise and Stand goals) — closing a ring treats your pet to a feast.
  • Sleep (when you were asleep) — so your pet learns your usual bedtime and sleeps when you do. Only the learned bedtime and wake time are kept.
  • Pippet only reads this data — it never writes to Apple Health.
  • This data is never transmitted off your device, never stored on any server, and never shared with us or anyone else.
  • You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Pippet still works without it.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — such as your pet, its needs, growth, name, personality and favourite treats, the species, treasures and past friends in your codex, a short daily summary (steps, workout minutes, care) used for the "Yesterday" card, and your settings — is saved locally on your device only.

This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is not accessible to us. If you delete the app, this locally stored data is removed with it.

Pet visits

On iPhone you can send your pet to visit a friend, and send a note back after a friend's pet has visited you. You choose who gets it and how to send it (Messages, for example). The link carries only the pet: its name, species and look, and for a note, how many strokes and treats it got. Nothing about you, and nothing from Apple Health.

The pet rides in the part of the link after the "#", which is never sent to any server, including ours. The visit page reads it on your friend's phone and stores nothing. A guest pet is kept on the phone that hosts it.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Pippet contains:

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No third‑party SDKs that collect data.
  • No accounts or sign‑in.

Pippet does not connect to the internet to collect, send, or receive any personal data.

Children's privacy

Pippet is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
