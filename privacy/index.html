Privacy Policy — Pippet

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Thank you for playing Pippet. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: Pippet does not collect or share any personal data. Your Health data never leaves your device. Your pet lives on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and we can't see any of it.

Data we collect

  • None. Pippet does not collect any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or any other personal details to play.

Health & activity data

With your permission, Pippet reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit), entirely on your device:

  • Step count — turned into food and energy for your virtual pet, and into little treasures it "finds" on your walks.
  • Workouts (start and end times only) — so your pet can cheer and "train" with you.
  • Activity rings (progress toward your Move, Exercise and Stand goals) — closing a ring treats your pet to a feast.
  • Sleep (when you were asleep) — so your pet learns your usual bedtime and sleeps when you do. Only the learned bedtime and wake time are kept.
  • Pippet only reads this data — it never writes to Apple Health.
  • This data — your steps, workouts, rings and sleep, and the daily totals and bedtime Pippet keeps from them — never leaves your device. It is never sent to us (we have no servers), never stored in iCloud, and never shared with anyone.
  • Only what your pet gets from it in the game (food, treasures, how sporty it grows up) becomes part of your game progress.
  • You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Pippet still works without it.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — such as your pet, its needs, growth, name, personality and favourite treats, the species, treasures and past friends in your codex, a short daily summary (steps, workout minutes, closed rings, care) used for the "Yesterday" card and your stats, and your settings — is saved on your device.

It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is not accessible to us. If you delete the app, the data on your device is removed with it.

iCloud backup

If you're signed in to iCloud, Pippet backs up your game progress to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage: your pet, the friends, treasures and milestones in your codex, which days you cared for your pet, and the language you chose. It contains no Health data: no steps, workouts, rings or sleep.

This is what keeps your pet safe on a new watch or after a reinstall, and how the same pet lives on both your Apple Watch and your iPhone. The backup is stored by Apple in your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. Deleting the app doesn't remove it, so your pet can come back if you reinstall.

Pet visits

On iPhone you can send your pet to visit a friend, and send a note back after a friend's pet has visited you. You choose who gets it and how to send it (Messages, for example). The link carries only the pet: its name, species and look, and for a note, how many strokes and treats it got. Nothing about you, and nothing from Apple Health.

The pet rides in the part of the link after the "#", which is never sent to any server, including ours. The visit page reads it on your friend's phone and stores nothing. A guest pet is kept on the phone that hosts it.

The visit page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy; the pet is never part of them.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Pippet contains:

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No third‑party SDKs that collect data.
  • No accounts or sign‑in.

Other than your iCloud backup, which Apple handles, Pippet does not connect to the internet to collect, send, or receive any data.

Children's privacy

Pippet is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
